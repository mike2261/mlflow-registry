# Open-weight Text-to-Speech for Vietnamese (state as of September 2026)

Scope note: Vietnamese only. Research window: web sources fetched 2026-09-26. "Open-weight" = weights downloadable from Hugging Face (HF) and self-hostable. Where a license forbids commercial use this is flagged explicitly because it decides whether a model can sit behind a production MLflow registry.

## Key Question 1: Which open-weight models support Vietnamese well today?

### Takeaway
As of September 2026 the practically usable Vietnamese options are almost all **Vietnamese-specific releases or Vietnamese fine-tunes of foreign base models** (VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo, Viterbox, F5-TTS Vietnamese fine-tunes, VietTTS, viXTTS, Valtec, IndexTTS-2-Vietnamese, plus the old MMS-TTS-vie and Piper voices). None of the headline open multilingual models (XTTS-v2, Chatterbox Multilingual v3, Fish Speech S1, CosyVoice 3, Qwen3-TTS, Kokoro, IndexTTS 2.5) list Vietnamese in their official language list; the only 2026 Alibaba model that added Vietnamese (Qwen-Audio-3.0-TTS) is API-only.

### Cited Findings

**Vietnamese-specific / fine-tuned models (open weights)**

- **VieNeu-TTS (pnnbao97 / pnnbao-ump)** — GitHub describes it as "Vietnamese TTS with instant voice cloning, on-device, real-time CPU inference, 48kHz audio quality"; current open release is **v3 Turbo** (PyTorch/ONNX, GPU/CPU, bilingual Vi-En, 48 kHz) with a **v3 Nano** preview (ONNX CPU-only, 24 kHz, 48M-param flow-matching model, lower quality); v1, v2 and the v2-Turbo/CPU variants are deprecated; **v4 is proprietary and only available through the vieneu.io API** — [GitHub pnnbao97/VieNeu-TTS](https://github.com/pnnbao97/VieNeu-TTS)
- VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo HF card: license **Apache-2.0**, **0.1B parameters**, BF16 safetensors plus ONNX (CPU) and PyTorch (GPU) exports, codec **MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer-Nano** (OpenMOSS, Apache-2.0), "trained from scratch (not a fine-tune)", **~10,000 hours of English–Vietnamese speech** ("detailed pipeline not publicly disclosed; dataset is gated"), 48 kHz, **23 preset voices covering North/Central/South** regions, voice cloning from 3–8 s clips, 634,482 downloads last month, no MOS/WER published — [HF pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo](https://huggingface.co/pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo)
- Earlier VieNeu versions: VieNeu-TTS (0.5B) was "fine-tuned from the NeuTTS Air architecture", VieNeu-TTS-0.3B was trained from scratch with "2x faster inference"; VieNeu-TTS-1000h was "trained on ~1000 hours of high-quality Vietnamese speech" with 24 kHz output — [HF pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS](https://huggingface.co/pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS); [HF VieNeu-TTS-v2 collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/pnnbao-ump/vieneu-tts-v2); [HF Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS)
- **Viterbox (iamdinhthuan/viterbox-tts, weights dolly-vn/viterbox)** — Chatterbox (Resemble AI) fine-tuned on **3,000+ hours of Vietnamese** (ViVoice ~1000 h, PhoAudiobook ~1000 h, Dolly-Audio ~1000 h); vocabulary expanded by 1,845 tokens (total 2,549); T3 backbone **520M parameters**; weights **CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial)**; zero-shot cloning from 3–10 s reference; ~6 GB VRAM for GPU inference, CPU "significantly slower"; "works best with fully diacritized text"; no RTF numbers, no release date, no MOS published — [GitHub iamdinhthuan/viterbox-tts](https://github.com/iamdinhthuan/viterbox-tts); [HF dolly-vn/viterbox](https://huggingface.co/dolly-vn/viterbox)
- **F5-TTS Vietnamese fine-tunes** (several, all based on SWivid/F5-TTS, DiT + ConvNeXt V2 text encoder, Vocos vocoder):
  - `hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice` (mirror `APITokenCloud/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice`): fine-tuned from F5-TTS_Base on **~1000 h** (viVoice + VLSP 2021/2022/2023), audio filtered to 1–30 s, background music removed with Demucs, transcripts filtered with Zalo AI's Chunk-Large-Former ASR, trained ~1.5 months on one RTX 3090 (batch 3200 frames), checkpoint `model_last.pt`, license **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 "Non-commercial research use only"**, 3,867 downloads last month, 23 Spaces — [HF hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice](https://huggingface.co/hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice); [GitHub nguyenthienhy/F5-TTS-Vietnamese](https://github.com/nguyenthienhy/F5-TTS-Vietnamese)
  - `hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-100h`: fine-tuned on ~150 h (VLSP 2021/22/23, VietTTS, TeacherDinh-UEH) — [HF hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-100h](https://huggingface.co/hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-100h)
  - `namprice227/F5-TTS-Vietnamese1000h-Pretrain`: F5-TTS **pretrained from scratch on 1000 h of PhoAudiobook**, plus a variant fine-tuned on 200 h of PhoAudiobook from the official F5-TTS Base — [HF namprice227/F5-TTS-Vietnamese1000h-Pretrain](https://huggingface.co/namprice227/F5-TTS-Vietnamese1000h-Pretrain); demo [HF Space nam194/F5-TTS-Vietnamese](https://huggingface.co/spaces/nam194/F5-TTS-Vietnamese)
  - `zalopay/vietnamese-tts` (Zalopay, 2025): F5-TTS fine-tune on "200+ hours public Vietnamese Voice and Youtube", license **CC-BY-4.0**, model dims 1024 / depth 22 / 16 heads / text dim 512, Vocos vocoder; self-reported limitations: "May struggle with uncommon words or names", "Limited support for different accents or dialects", background noise / pronunciation inconsistencies, duplicated-voice artifacts; **no WER/MOS/similarity numbers** — [HF zalopay/vietnamese-tts](https://huggingface.co/zalopay/vietnamese-tts)
  - `erax-ai/EraX-Smile-Female-F5-V1.0`: F5-TTS fine-tune, single Vietnamese female voice, 800k+ samples including a 500 h private set, **CC-BY-NC-4.0**, online zero-shot cloning, training incomplete at time of card (~420k of 1M planned steps); a male voice and a Piper-based Raspberry-Pi model were on the roadmap — [HF erax-ai/EraX-Smile-Female-F5-V1.0](https://huggingface.co/erax-ai/EraX-Smile-Female-F5-V1.0)
  - Other community F5 checkpoints exist (`yukiakai/F5-TTS-Vietnamese`, `danhtran2mind/Vi-F5-TTS`, Space `toandev/F5-TTS-Vietnamese`); details not verified — [HF search results](https://huggingface.co/yukiakai/F5-TTS-Vietnamese), [HF danhtran2mind/Vi-F5-TTS](https://huggingface.co/danhtran2mind/Vi-F5-TTS)
- **VietTTS (dangvansam/viet-tts)** — "An Open-Source Vietnamese Text to Speech"; code Apache-2.0, **pre-trained models and audio CC BY-NC**, "based on an in-the-wild dataset"; code "borrowed from CosyVoice"; 24 built-in voices (mix of male/female incl. celebrity-named presets such as son-tung-mtp), voice cloning from a prompt audio file, **OpenAI-compatible /v1/audio/speech API with streaming**, text normalization with **Vinorm**, silero-vad, Python >= 3.10, Docker/NVIDIA; Linux only at time of README ("Windows and macOS support coming soon"); no parameter count, hours, latency or eval numbers published — [GitHub dangvansam/viet-tts](https://github.com/dangvansam/viet-tts); [HF dangvansam/viet-tts](https://huggingface.co/dangvansam/viet-tts)
- **viXTTS (capleaf/viXTTS; also thinhlpg/viXTTS)** — fine-tuned from **XTTS-v2.0.3** with an expanded tokenizer on the viVoice dataset (888k entries); license **Coqui Public Model License (CPML)**; 6 s reference for cloning; card states "This model is only fine-tuned in Vietnamese", other languages untested; **"Subpar performance for Vietnamese sentences under 10 words, producing inconsistent output and odd trailing sounds"**; "Incompatibility with the original TTS library"; 601 downloads last month, used by 87 Spaces — [HF capleaf/viXTTS](https://huggingface.co/capleaf/viXTTS)
- **Valtec-TTS (tronghieuit/valtec-tts, "V-TTS")** — "The lightest Vietnamese TTS with Multi-Speaker TTS and Zero-Shot Voice Cloning", no GPU required; **74.8M params** (speaker encoder 8.03M, style encoder 7.80M, prosody predictor 2.52M, synthesizer 56.45M), ~285 MB FP32; **5 built-in voices: Northern/Southern x Male/Female** (NF, SF, NM1, SM, NM2) plus 6 reference voices for cloning; 24 kHz; 3–10 s reference for cloning; **CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial)**; v1.0.5 April 2026 with a Windows executable — [GitHub tronghieuit/valtec-tts](https://github.com/tronghieuit/valtec-tts)
- **IndexTTS-2 Vietnamese (dinhthuan/index-tts-2-vietnamese)** — base IndexTeam/IndexTTS-2 (GPT semantic generator + S2Mel + BigVGAN), Vietnamese primary while retaining English/Chinese ("no catastrophic forgetting observed"), **self-reported MOS ~4.3 on an internal Vietnamese validation set**, 22.05 kHz, <=15 s speaker prompt, "Not robust to noisy transcripts; clean text is required", released June 23 2025; card says Apache-2.0 **but also "Commercial use requires permission from IndexTTS authors"** (conflicting) — [HF dinhthuan/index-tts-2-vietnamese](https://huggingface.co/dinhthuan/index-tts-2-vietnamese)
- **Meta MMS-TTS vie (facebook/mms-tts-vie)** — VITS, **36.3M params** (F32), HiFi-GAN-style decoder, license **CC-BY-NC 4.0**, runs via `transformers.VitsModel` (Transformers >= 4.33), stochastic duration predictor (non-deterministic without a fixed seed); single voice, part of MMS 1000+ language project (2023) — [HF facebook/mms-tts-vie](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-tts-vie)
- **Piper vi_VN voices (rhasspy/piper-voices)** — three voices: `vi_VN-25hours_single-low`, `vi_VN-vais1000-medium`, `vi_VN-vivos-x_low` — [GitHub rhasspy/piper VOICES.md](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/blob/master/VOICES.md); [HF rhasspy/piper-voices vi/vi_VN](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/v1.0.0/vi/vi_VN). The vais1000 medium model card: dataset "VAIS 1000 – Vietnamese Speech Synthesis Corpus" (IEEE DataPort, **CC-BY 4.0**), "Finetuned from U.S. English lessac voice (medium quality)", 22,050 Hz; repository header shows MIT — [HF MODEL_CARD](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/vi/vi_VN/vais1000/medium/MODEL_CARD). A repackaged version exists at [speaches-ai/piper-vi_VN-25hours_single-low](https://huggingface.co/speaches-ai/piper-vi_VN-25hours_single-low).
- Older VITS-based Vietnamese Spaces exist (e.g. `fhieni/Vietnamese_VITS_TTS` using viphoneme) — [HF Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/fhieni/Vietnamese_VITS_TTS/blob/72fb86d8e1e4e5afebe0b1806ce8b0f33ea51dfe/README.md)

- **XTTS-v2 (coqui/XTTS-v2)**: 17 languages (en, es, fr, de, it, pt, pl, tr, ru, nl, cs, ar, zh-cn, ja, hu, ko, hi) — **Vietnamese not included**; Coqui Public Model License; 24 kHz — [HF coqui/XTTS-v2](https://huggingface.co/coqui/XTTS-v2)
- **Chatterbox Multilingual (ResembleAI/chatterbox, V3 latest)**: 23 languages (Arabic, Danish, German, Greek, English, Spanish, Finnish, French, Hebrew, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Dutch, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Swedish, Swahili, Turkish, Chinese) — **Vietnamese not included**; 0.5B params; MIT; Perth watermark on all outputs — [HF ResembleAI/chatterbox](https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox); Resemble's V3 post describes "21 languages and 4 dialects" and MIT license — [Resemble AI blog](https://www.resemble.ai/resources/chatterbox-multilingual-v3-tts-with-embedded-watermarking-for-25-languages). - **CosyVoice 3 (Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512)**: 9 languages (zh, en, ja, ko, de, es, fr, it, ru) + 18 Chinese dialects — **no Vietnamese**; open Vietnamese requests remain in issues #970 and #1502 — [HF FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512](https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512); [CosyVoice issue #1502](https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice/issues/1502); [issue #970](https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice/issues/970). - **Qwen3-TTS (open weights, Apache-2.0)**: 10 languages (zh, en, ja, ko, de, fr, ru, pt, es, it) — **no Vietnamese**; a March 20 2026 GitHub discussion asking for Vietnamese has no maintainer reply — [GitHub Qwen3-TTS discussion #274](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS/discussions/274); [HF discussion "Add Vietnamese language"](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice/discussions/27); [GitHub QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS)
- **Qwen-Audio-3.0-TTS** (released July 20 2026) adds Vietnamese among 16 languages but is a **hosted, API-only** model (Flash/Plus tiers), not open weights — [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/20/alibabas-tongyi-lab-releases-qwen-audio-3-0-tts-a-hosted-text-to-speech-model-in-flash-and-plus-tiers-across-16-languages/); [Alibaba Cloud blog](https://www.alibabacloud.com/blog/qwen-audio-3-0-tts-more-multilingual-easier-to-direct_603379)
- **Kokoro-82M**: Apache-2.0, 82M params, "8 languages & 54 voices" in v1.0; Vietnamese not among them (card lists languages in VOICES.md; Vietnamese was not mentioned) — [HF hexgrad/Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)
- **IndexTTS 2.5**: Chinese, English, Japanese, Spanish, Arabic — no Vietnamese — [IndexTTS 2.5 tech report](https://index-tts.github.io/index-tts2-5.github.io/)
- Broad community tooling (ComfyUI TTS-Audio-Suite) integrates Qwen3-TTS, CosyVoice 3, IndexTTS-2, Chatterbox, F5-TTS, Higgs Audio 2/3, VibeVoice, but this says nothing about Vietnamese support — [GitHub diodiogod/TTS-Audio-Suite](https://github.com/diodiogod/TTS-Audio-Suite)

**Datasets underpinning most Vietnamese models**

- **viVoice (capleaf/viVoice)**: 887,772 samples, **1,016.97 hours**, 24 kHz, scraped from **186 YouTube channels**, 61.3% +/- 3.02% male, estimated transcription error 1.8% +/- 0.82%, license **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, research only** — [HF datasets/capleaf/viVoice](https://huggingface.co/datasets/capleaf/viVoice)
- **PhoAudiobook (thivux/phoaudiobook)**: 941 hours, 735 speakers (capped at 4 h/speaker), curated from 23K hours of 2,697 publicly available Vietnamese audiobooks; released "for non-commercial use" (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 icon on arXiv page); paper "Zero-Shot Text-to-Speech for Vietnamese" (ACL 2025 short; authors Thi Vu, Linh The Nguyen, Dat Quoc Nguyen) fine-tuned VALL-E, VoiceCraft and XTTS-v2 on it and reports "VALL-E and VoiceCraft show superior performance in synthesizing short sentences" — [arXiv 2506.01322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01322); [ACL Anthology 2025.acl-short.81](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-short.81/); [dataset listing](https://metatext.io/datasets/thivux/phoaudiobook)
- **VLSP 2021/2022/2023 TTS data** is used by the hynt F5 fine-tunes — [HF hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice](https://huggingface.co/hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice)

### Inferences
- The only Vietnamese model with an unambiguous permissive weights license *and* a modern architecture *and* published latency numbers is **VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo (Apache-2.0)**; its 634k monthly downloads suggest it is the de-facto default in the Vietnamese community in 2026. Its weakness for a registry use-case is that the training data is gated/undisclosed and no MOS/WER is published.
- **zalopay/vietnamese-tts (CC-BY-4.0)** is the only F5-TTS-family Vietnamese checkpoint with a commercially usable license; every other F5 Vietnamese fine-tune (hynt, EraX) is CC-BY-NC(-SA) because it inherits viVoice/private data restrictions.
- Viterbox is probably the highest-data-volume Vietnamese model (3,000 h) but is CC BY-NC 4.0 and inherits scraped-YouTube (viVoice) and audiobook (PhoAudiobook) data; unsuitable for commercial deployment without re-licensing.
- "Vietnamese fine-tunes of X" (viXTTS, Viterbox, index-tts-2-vietnamese, F5 fine-tunes) are the *only* route to Vietnamese from the big multilingual base models; the base models themselves will produce garbage or refuse Vietnamese input.

### Gaps
- No TTS Arena / public leaderboard with Vietnamese samples was found; all naturalness claims are self-reported (only index-tts-2-vietnamese gives a MOS, ~4.3, on an internal set).
- Higgs Audio (v2/v3) and Kyutai TTS: no source confirming or denying Vietnamese support was found; treat as unsupported.
- No official open-weight TTS releases from VinAI, FPT AI or Viettel were found; VinAI released the PhoAudiobook *dataset* (non-commercial) but the search found no checkpoint release. Zalo's only open release is zalopay/vietnamese-tts. Zalo AI's own commercial TTS reportedly offers 4 voices (2 Northern, 2 Southern) but that is a closed product — [Viettel AI listing](https://viettelai.vn/en/tin-tuc/top-5-phan-mem-doc-van-ban-thanh-giong-noi-mien-phi) (secondary source).

## Key Question 2: Per-model specs — naturalness, tones/diacritics, accents, size, license, cloning, streaming, latency, HF id, format, stack

### Takeaway
Concrete, published numbers exist only for VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo (RTF, TTFA, concurrency) and Valtec (RTF on CPU/GPU); everyone else publishes hours of data and a license but no latency or MOS. Northern-vs-Southern accent selection is explicit only in Valtec (5 N/S voices) and VieNeu (North/Central/South presets); other models get accent only via the cloning reference.

Additional per-model facts:
- VieNeu v3 Turbo supports **up to 16 concurrent streams on an RTX 3060** (185 ms median TTFA, 0.59 RTF per stream) and ~1–2 concurrent streams on CPU; int8 CPU path **requires VNNI**, "older CPUs may produce garbled audio"; supports LoRA fine-tuning, multi-speaker conversation mode, and experimental emotion cues `[cười]`, `[thở dài]`, `[hắng giọng]`; text normalization/phonemization via **sea-g2p** — [VieNeu GitHub](https://github.com/pnnbao97/VieNeu-TTS)
- VieNeu v3 Nano is "noticeably lower quality (especially English / code-switched En-Vi text)" — [VieNeu GitHub](https://github.com/pnnbao97/VieNeu-TTS)
- VieNeu v3 Turbo card says voice cloning "requires user consent" and the style/reading-style argument is deprecated — [VieNeu v3 Turbo HF](https://huggingface.co/pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo)
- Valtec: Windows executable ~394 MB incl. PyTorch, Docker image 2–3 GB; "cannot improve upon reference audio quality"; multilingual output "with reduced fidelity" — [Valtec GitHub](https://github.com/tronghieuit/valtec-tts)
- MMS-TTS vie: output non-deterministic without a fixed seed due to stochastic duration predictor — [MMS-TTS vie HF](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-tts-vie)
- viXTTS trained on viVoice 888k entries; card carries a "configuration parsing warning" (config.json formatting) — [viXTTS HF](https://huggingface.co/capleaf/viXTTS)

### Inferences
- For a Python service behind MLflow with weights in MinIO, VieNeu v3 Turbo is the easiest to package: safetensors + ONNX files, a pip-installable package, torch-free CPU path, and a documented OpenAI-compatible server; VietTTS is the next easiest (OpenAI-compatible, Docker) but is CC BY-NC.
- F5-TTS-family checkpoints are single `.pt`/safetensors files plus a vocab file and depend on the F5-TTS repo and a Vocos vocoder download at runtime; these need to be vendored into MinIO too for reproducible serving.
- viXTTS requires a *forked* TTS library, which is a packaging liability (Coqui TTS upstream is archived, and the card states incompatibility).
- Realistic GPU latency for the F5/Chatterbox/IndexTTS class on Vietnamese is not published; assume several hundred ms to >1 s TTFA for non-streaming DiT/AR pipelines (inference; not measured in any source found).

### Gaps
- No published MOS/CMOS, WER (via Vietnamese ASR) or speaker-similarity numbers for VieNeu, Viterbox, VietTTS, viXTTS, Valtec, zalopay or hynt models; only index-tts-2-vietnamese reports a self-measured MOS ~4.3.
- Download sizes (GB) are not stated on most cards; parameter counts for VietTTS and hynt F5 checkpoints are not stated (F5-TTS Base is ~336M per upstream, not verified here).
- Sample rate for Viterbox, VietTTS, hynt/zalopay F5 fine-tunes not stated on their cards.

## Key Question 3: How do the Vietnamese fine-tunes compare with the big multilingual models on Vietnamese? Numbers, abbreviations, English loanwords

### Takeaway
There is no apples-to-apples benchmark; the only academic comparison (PhoAudiobook / ACL 2025) fine-tuned VALL-E, VoiceCraft and XTTS-v2 and found the AR models (VALL-E, VoiceCraft) better on short sentences. Text normalization is almost entirely external: VietTTS uses Vinorm, VieNeu uses sea-g2p, hynt trained on lowercased normalized text, and a 2026 paper (VietNormalizer, MIT) exists precisely because most Vietnamese TTS models do not handle numbers, dates, currency, acronyms and loanwords natively. Only VieNeu explicitly advertises Vi-En code-switching.

### Cited Findings
- "Zero-Shot Text-to-Speech for Vietnamese" (ACL 2025 short) fine-tuned **VALL-E, VoiceCraft and XTTS-V2** on PhoAudiobook; "PhoAudiobook consistently enhances model performance across various metrics", and "VALL-E and VoiceCraft show superior performance in synthesizing short sentences" — [arXiv 2506.01322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01322); [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-short.81/)
- viXTTS (XTTS-v2 fine-tune) has "subpar performance for Vietnamese sentences under 10 words, producing inconsistent output and odd trailing sounds" — [viXTTS HF](https://huggingface.co/capleaf/viXTTS)
- VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo: bilingual En–Vi code-switching, "seamless English integration within Vietnamese sentences", podcast-style multi-speaker scripts — [VieNeu v3 Turbo HF](https://huggingface.co/pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo); [VieNeu-TTS-v2 collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/pnnbao-ump/vieneu-tts-v2)
- VieNeu v3 Nano is weaker specifically on "English / code-switched En-Vi text" — [VieNeu GitHub](https://github.com/pnnbao97/VieNeu-TTS)
- VietTTS uses **Vinorm** for text normalization (numbers/abbreviations) before synthesis — [VietTTS GitHub](https://github.com/dangvansam/viet-tts)
- hynt F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice was trained on "normalized, lowercase text with standard punctuation preserved", i.e. the model expects pre-normalized input — [hynt HF](https://huggingface.co/hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice)
- zalopay/vietnamese-tts self-reports "May struggle with uncommon words or names" — [zalopay HF](https://huggingface.co/zalopay/vietnamese-tts)
- Viterbox "works best with fully diacritized text" (Hỗ trợ tốt nhất với văn bản có dấu đầy đủ) — [Viterbox GitHub](https://github.com/iamdinhthuan/viterbox-tts)
- index-tts-2-vietnamese: "Not robust to noisy transcripts; clean text is required" — [HF](https://huggingface.co/dinhthuan/index-tts-2-vietnamese)
- **VietNormalizer** (arXiv 2603.04145, March 2026; MIT; `pip install vietnormalizer`) is a dependency-free rule-based Vietnamese TTS normalizer handling (1) integers/decimals/large numbers to words, (2) dates and times, (3) VND and USD currency, (4) percentages, (5) acronyms via a customizable CSV dictionary, (6) transliteration of non-Vietnamese loanwords to Vietnamese phonetic approximations, (7) Unicode normalization / emoji removal; the authors argue existing solutions "require heavy neural dependencies while covering only a narrow subset of NSW classes, or are embedded within larger NLP toolkits" — [arXiv 2603.04145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04145)
- The multilingual base models do not list Vietnamese at all (XTTS-v2, Chatterbox Multilingual V3, Fish Speech S1, CosyVoice 3, Qwen3-TTS, Kokoro, IndexTTS 2.5) — see Q1 citations.

### Inferences
- Because none of the big multilingual open models officially support Vietnamese, "fine-tune vs multilingual" is not a real choice today: the fine-tunes *are* the only way to use those architectures for Vietnamese. The relevant comparison is between fine-tune families (Chatterbox-based Viterbox vs F5-based vs from-scratch VieNeu), for which no shared benchmark exists.
- Numbers/abbreviations: every model surveyed except VieNeu relies on an external normalizer (Vinorm or VietNormalizer) or on the caller pre-normalizing text; a serving layer should run VietNormalizer (MIT) or Vinorm before the model regardless of which model is chosen.
- English loanwords: VieNeu is the only model that markets code-switching; F5 and XTTS fine-tunes trained on lowercased Vietnamese will tend to read English words letter-by-letter or with Vietnamese phonotactics (inference from training-data description, not measured).

### Gaps
- No quantitative comparison (WER/MOS) between VieNeu, Viterbox, F5 fine-tunes, VietTTS and viXTTS was found anywhere (HF, arXiv, Reddit, Vietnamese blogs); the query for Vietnamese-language comparisons ("so sánh ... đánh giá 2026") returned no comparative reviews.
- No source quantifies tone-error rates or diacritics robustness for any model.
- VLSP 2025 had no TTS shared task (it had Speech Quality Assessment and Voice Conversion tasks), so no recent shared-task ranking exists — [VLSP 2025 proceedings](https://aclanthology.org/2025.vlsp-1.0.pdf); [VLSP 2025 SQA task](https://aclanthology.org/2025.vlsp-1.7.pdf); [VLSP 2025 VC task](https://aclanthology.org/2025.vlsp-1.13/)

## Key Question 4: Known gotchas — wrong tones, missing normalization, dataset licensing, non-commercial licenses

### Takeaway
The dominant gotcha is licensing: most Vietnamese checkpoints are CC BY-NC(-SA) because they are trained on viVoice (YouTube scrape, CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0) or PhoAudiobook (audiobooks, non-commercial), or on undisclosed "in-the-wild" data; only VieNeu v3 Turbo (Apache-2.0), zalopay/vietnamese-tts (CC-BY-4.0) and the Piper vais1000 voice (MIT/CC-BY data) are clearly commercial-safe. Second gotcha: short sentences and un-normalized text (numbers, acronyms) degrade several models, and diacritics-free input is unsupported.

### Cited Findings

**Licensing / dataset provenance**
- viVoice: "over 1000 hours of high-quality, cleaned audio from YouTube ... from 186 YouTube channels", **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, research purposes only** — [HF datasets/capleaf/viVoice](https://huggingface.co/datasets/capleaf/viVoice). Downstream: viXTTS (CPML), hynt F5 (CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0), Viterbox (CC BY-NC 4.0).
- PhoAudiobook: curated from 23K hours of 2,697 publicly available audiobooks, released **for non-commercial use** (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) — [arXiv 2506.01322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01322). Downstream: namprice227 F5 pretrain, Viterbox.
- VietTTS: models "CC BY-NC License, based on an in-the-wild dataset"; presets named after real people (e.g. son-tung-mtp) and README disclaimer "for academic purposes", content "sourced partially from the internet" — [VietTTS HF](https://huggingface.co/dangvansam/viet-tts); [VietTTS GitHub](https://github.com/dangvansam/viet-tts)
- EraX F5: CC-BY-NC-4.0, and card notes the base F5-TTS was trained on Emilia "which is under BY-NC 4.0 license" — [EraX HF](https://huggingface.co/erax-ai/EraX-Smile-Female-F5-V1.0)
- hynt F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice: "CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 — Non-commercial research use only" — [hynt HF](https://huggingface.co/hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice)
- Valtec: "CC BY-NC 4.0 — Non-commercial use only. Commercial applications require explicit written permission" — [Valtec GitHub](https://github.com/tronghieuit/valtec-tts)
- Viterbox weights: "CC BY-NC 4.0 – non-commercial use only" — [Viterbox GitHub](https://github.com/iamdinhthuan/viterbox-tts)
- MMS-TTS vie: CC-BY-NC 4.0 — [HF facebook/mms-tts-vie](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-tts-vie)
- viXTTS / XTTS-v2: Coqui Public Model License (a non-commercial license; the card itself does not spell out commercial terms) — [viXTTS HF](https://huggingface.co/capleaf/viXTTS); [XTTS-v2 HF](https://huggingface.co/coqui/XTTS-v2)
- index-tts-2-vietnamese: card states Apache-2.0 but also "Commercial use requires permission from IndexTTS authors" — an internal contradiction — [HF](https://huggingface.co/dinhthuan/index-tts-2-vietnamese)
- Fish Speech: "FISH AUDIO RESEARCH LICENSE" (irrelevant for Vietnamese since unsupported) — [fish-speech GitHub](https://github.com/fishaudio/fish-speech)
- Commercial-safe: VieNeu v3 Turbo **Apache-2.0** (but training data gated/undisclosed) — [HF](https://huggingface.co/pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo); zalopay/vietnamese-tts **CC-BY-4.0** (trained on "public Vietnamese Voice and Youtube", so provenance of the YouTube portion is itself unclear) — [HF](https://huggingface.co/zalopay/vietnamese-tts); Piper vais1000 voice trained on the CC-BY 4.0 VAIS-1000 corpus in an MIT repo — [MODEL_CARD](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/vi/vi_VN/vais1000/medium/MODEL_CARD)

**Quality / behaviour gotchas**
- viXTTS: inconsistent output and trailing sounds for sentences under 10 words; incompatible with upstream TTS library — [viXTTS HF](https://huggingface.co/capleaf/viXTTS)
- Short-sentence weakness of XTTS-v2-style models is corroborated by the PhoAudiobook paper (AR models VALL-E/VoiceCraft better on short sentences) — [arXiv 2506.01322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01322)
- zalopay: duplicated voice artifacts, noise/pronunciation inconsistencies, weak on uncommon words/names and dialects — [zalopay HF](https://huggingface.co/zalopay/vietnamese-tts)
- Viterbox needs fully diacritized text and clean, noise-free reference audio; ~6 GB VRAM — [Viterbox GitHub](https://github.com/iamdinhthuan/viterbox-tts)
- VieNeu: int8 CPU path requires VNNI or "older CPUs may produce garbled audio"; Nano variant weak on English/code-switch; emotion cues experimental; v4 (best cloning fidelity) is closed — [VieNeu GitHub](https://github.com/pnnbao97/VieNeu-TTS)
- MMS-TTS vie is non-deterministic without a seed and is a single 2023-era VITS voice — [HF](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-tts-vie)
- Text normalization is not built into most models; VietNormalizer's authors state existing normalizers cover only a narrow subset of non-standard-word classes — [arXiv 2603.04145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04145)
- Chatterbox (and therefore Viterbox, if the watermarker is retained) embeds an imperceptible Perth watermark in every output — [ResembleAI/chatterbox HF](https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox)

### Inferences
- For a self-hosted production registry, the shortlist that avoids NC licenses is: **VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo** (best quality/latency evidence, Apache-2.0), **zalopay/vietnamese-tts** (F5, CC-BY-4.0, weaker self-reported quality), **Piper vi_VN-vais1000-medium** (tiny CPU fallback, older VITS quality). Everything else (Viterbox, hynt F5, EraX, VietTTS, Valtec, viXTTS, MMS-TTS) is non-commercial or CPML.
- Even for Apache-2.0 VieNeu, the undisclosed ~10k-hour dataset is a due-diligence risk; the model author also sells a closed v4 API, so open releases may lag the commercial version.
- Any deployment should pre-normalize text (VietNormalizer, MIT) and enforce diacritized input, because models were trained on normalized/diacritized transcripts.

### Gaps
- No source documents tone-error or wrong-diacritic failure rates; "wrong tones" complaints could not be substantiated with citable measurements.
- No legal analysis was found regarding whether models trained on scraped YouTube (viVoice) or audiobooks (PhoAudiobook) can be relicensed permissively; the licenses on the datasets are the only evidence.
- Reddit/Facebook Vietnamese-community comparisons of these models were not surfaced by search; anecdotal quality rankings could not be cited.
