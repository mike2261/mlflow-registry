# Open-weight English TTS models (state as of September 2026)

Scope: open-weight (downloadable, self-hostable) TTS models for English, to be served from a Python service with weights pulled from Hugging Face and registered in MLflow. Vietnamese is covered by another researcher. Multilingual support noted only briefly.

## Key Question 1: Which open-weight TTS models lead English quality rankings right now?

### Takeaway
On the Artificial Analysis Speech Arena (the most-cited blind-listening board in 2026), the open-weight top tier is Breeze TTS 2 (Aug 2026), Fish Audio S2 Pro (Mar 2026), Step Audio EditX, Voxtral TTS (Mar 2026), NVIDIA Magpie-Multilingual 357M and Kokoro 82M v1.0 -- but the two highest-ranked (Breeze, Fish S2 Pro) plus Voxtral carry non-commercial weight licenses, so the best *commercially usable* leaders are Step Audio EditX (Apache 2.0, with a license-ambiguity caveat), Kokoro (Apache 2.0) and Chatterbox (MIT). Every top-10 model on that board is closed-source.

### Inferences
- For an English-only, commercially deployed self-hosted service, the arena evidence points to a shortlist of Kokoro (fast/cheap, no cloning), Chatterbox / Chatterbox-Turbo (MIT, cloning, watermark) and Step Audio EditX (Apache 2.0 code, highest permissive Elo) at the "quality" end, with Qwen3-TTS-1.7B and Fun-CosyVoice3 as strong Apache-2.0 options that lack arena scores but have very low published WER.
- Breeze TTS 2, Fish S2 Pro, Voxtral TTS and Higgs TTS 3 are the quality leaders but are only usable for internal evaluation/research unless a commercial license is bought.
- The two arenas (Artificial Analysis vs. HF TTS Arena V2) are not directly comparable (different Elo baselines and model pools); Artificial Analysis has far more 2026 open-weight coverage.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve the full HF TTS Arena V2 table (the API fetch surfaced only 3 open-source entries); no HF-Arena ranks for Chatterbox, Fish, Qwen3-TTS, etc.
- No published MOS/CMOS for Breeze TTS 2, Fish S2 Pro, Voxtral TTS, Step Audio EditX; only arena Elo.
- No arena ranking for Qwen3-TTS, Fun-CosyVoice3, MOSS-TTS v1.5, Dia2, IndexTTS-2/2.5, Kyutai TTS, Hume TADA, Orpheus, Sesame CSM.

## Key Question 2: Per-model fact sheet (score, size, license, cloning, streaming, latency, HF repo, format, stack)

### Takeaway
Licenses split cleanly into three buckets: permissive (Apache 2.0 / MIT: Kokoro, Chatterbox family, Qwen3-TTS, Fun-CosyVoice3, MOSS-TTS v1.5, Step Audio EditX, Dia/Dia2, Orpheus, Sesame CSM, Zonos, VibeVoice, Higgs Audio v2, StyleTTS 2, MeloTTS, Maya1, VoxCPM2, GLM-TTS), attribution/other-open (Kyutai TTS CC-BY 4.0, Magpie NVIDIA Open Model License, TADA Llama 3.2 Community License), and non-commercial (Breeze TTS 2, Fish S2 Pro, Voxtral TTS, Higgs TTS 3, F5-TTS, Spark-TTS, Llasa, XTTS-v2, IndexTTS by default). Latency numbers below are vendor-published on H100/H200-class hardware unless stated.

### Inferences
- Download size can be estimated from parameter count and dtype where not published: 82M fp32 ~= 330 MB (Kokoro; the "82MB" figure in the OfflineTTS article is almost certainly a params/bytes confusion), 0.5B bf16 ~= 1 GB (Chatterbox, CosyVoice3), 1.7B bf16 ~= 3.4 GB (Qwen3-TTS), 3B bf16 ~= 6 GB (Breeze, Step Audio EditX), 4-5B bf16 ~= 8-10 GB (Voxtral, Fish S2 Pro, Higgs TTS 3), 8B bf16 ~= 16 GB (MOSS-TTS v1.5). These are arithmetic estimates, not published numbers.
- Almost all 2025-2026 LLM-based models ship as safetensors and are loaded via `transformers` or a custom repo; only Kokoro (ONNX), VoxCPM2/Fish S2 Pro/TADA (community GGUF) have non-PyTorch runtime paths.

### Gaps
- Exact download sizes (GB) are not published on most model cards; only parameter counts and dtypes.
- No CPU latency numbers found for any model except Kokoro (and Chatterbox-Nano's "3x real-time on 8 cores" claim).
- IndexTTS-2 weight license full text not retrievable (404); only the "contact for commercial use" statement.
- OpenAudio S1 Mini and Maya1 licenses/params not verified from primary model cards.
- Parler-TTS, Piper, and Higgs Audio v2 were not verified from primary sources in this pass (Modal blog lists Higgs v2 as Apache 2.0).

## Key Question 3: Which are practical for a production Python service (dependency weight, stability, memory)?

### Takeaway
For a self-hosted Python service with MLflow/MinIO-managed weights, the low-risk production choices are Kokoro-82M (tiny, ONNX/CPU-capable, Apache 2.0, but no cloning and 512-token chunks), Chatterbox-Turbo/Multilingual (MIT, pip-installable, cloning, sub-200 ms, but watermarked), and Qwen3-TTS-1.7B or Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B (Apache 2.0, `transformers`/vLLM, streaming, cloning, very low WER). The arena-leading 3-5B models require 12-24 GB GPUs and SGLang-Omni/vLLM-Omni serving stacks, and most of them are non-commercial.

### Cited Findings
- "There is still no Ollama for TTS" -- vLLM-Omni, LocalAI and llama.cpp-omni each support different model subsets — [Pinggy blog, Aug 26 2026](https://pinggy.io/blog/best_open_source_self_hosted_text_to_speech_models/)
- Kokoro: <2 GB VRAM or ~4 GB RAM via ONNX; ~0.03 RTF on A100; ~2.4x real-time on CPU; runs in browser via WebGPU/WASM — [Pinggy blog](https://pinggy.io/blog/best_open_source_self_hosted_text_to_speech_models/); [OfflineTTS](https://offlinetts.com/blog/tts-arena-leaderboard-2026/). Kokoro-FastAPI: prebuilt Docker images for CPU (amd64+arm64), CUDA 12.6/12.8, ROCm (experimental), Apple MPS; unloading reclaims 758 MB (short) to 1.6 GB (long-form) VRAM with ~5 s reload — [remsky/Kokoro-FastAPI](https://github.com/remsky/Kokoro-FastAPI)
- Kokoro "512 token context window, which usually amounts to about 30 seconds of audio"; text past 510 phonemes errors and must be chunked; keep chunks under ~500 characters, prosody degrades on longer passages — [deAPI Kokoro guide](https://deapi.ai/blog/kokoro-tts-guide-how-to-control-41-voices-with-nothing-but-punctuation); [FluidAudio issue #940](https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio/issues/940)
- Chatterbox: `pip install chatterbox-tts`, Python 3.11+, tested on Debian 11; Turbo single-step decoder for "Lower Compute and VRAM"; Nano runs 3x real-time on 8 CPU cores — [resemble-ai/chatterbox](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox); [chatterbox-tts on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/chatterbox-tts/)
- Qwen3-TTS: native `transformers` + optional FlashAttention-2, vLLM-Omni for offline batch (streaming in vLLM-Omni "coming later"); 0.6B and 1.7B checkpoints; bf16 — [QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS)
- Fun-CosyVoice3: vLLM and TensorRT-LLM Triton runtimes; text-in/audio-out streaming at ~150 ms — [FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512](https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512)
- Step Audio EditX: 12 GB GPU optimal (L40S tested), 6-8 GB with AWQ 4-bit, vLLM since Jan 23 2026; Linux/CUDA only; 30 s per-inference cap — [stepfun-ai/Step-Audio-EditX](https://huggingface.co/stepfun-ai/Step-Audio-EditX)
- Breeze TTS 2: 12 GB min / 24 GB recommended; Docker defaults to H100/Hopper, A100 compatible — [BreezeBlue/Breeze-TTS-2](https://huggingface.co/BreezeBlue/Breeze-TTS-2)
- Voxtral TTS: single GPU >= 16 GB; vLLM-Omni >= 0.18.0 recommended — [mistralai/Voxtral-4B-TTS-2603](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Voxtral-4B-TTS-2603)
- Fish S2 Pro / Higgs TTS 3 require SGLang-Omni (Higgs also vLLM-Omni) with OpenAI-compatible `/v1/audio/speech` — [fishaudio/s2-pro](https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/s2-pro); [bosonai/higgs-audio-v3-tts-4b](https://huggingface.co/bosonai/higgs-audio-v3-tts-4b)
- MOSS-TTS v1.5 is 8B and needs `transformers>=5.0.0`; CPU inference possible in fp32 — [OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-v1.5](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-v1.5)
- Sesame CSM-1B uses transformers >= 4.52.1 with CUDA graphs/static cache — [sesame/csm-1b](https://huggingface.co/sesame/csm-1b)
- Kyutai TTS ships a Python web server and a Rust server via the Moshi library — [kyutai/tts-1.6b-en_fr](https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-1.6b-en_fr)
- Maintenance signals (Pinggy, Aug 2026): Kokoro repo frozen (ecosystem via external maintainers); Zonos dormant since Feb 2025; Step Audio EditX upstream dormant since Apr 2026; Qwen3-TTS quiet since Mar 2026; GLM-TTS quiet since Apr 2026; VoxCPM2 actively developed — [Pinggy blog](https://pinggy.io/blog/best_open_source_self_hosted_text_to_speech_models/)
- XTTS-v2: "project abandoned 2024" — [BentoML blog](https://www.bentoml.com/blog/exploring-the-world-of-open-source-text-to-speech-models)
- Inferless L4 (24 GB) test, Jun 2025: Kokoro <0.3 s for all lengths; F5-TTS sub-7 s; Llama-OuteTTS-1.0-1B >4 min for 200 words; quality "excellent" for Kokoro, csm-1b, Spark-TTS, Orpheus-3b, F5-TTS, Llasa-3B — [Inferless](https://www.inferless.com/learn/comparing-different-text-to-speech---tts--models-part-2)

### Inferences
- Tiering for the MLflow registry: (a) CPU/edge tier: Kokoro-82M ONNX or Chatterbox-Nano; (b) single mid-range GPU (8-12 GB) tier: Chatterbox-Turbo/Multilingual, Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B, Qwen3-TTS-0.6B/1.7B; (c) 16-24 GB GPU "quality" tier: Step Audio EditX (Apache 2.0) or, for internal/non-commercial eval only, Breeze TTS 2 / Fish S2 Pro / Voxtral / Higgs TTS 3.
- Models whose reference stack is SGLang-Omni or vLLM-Omni (Fish, Higgs, Voxtral, Breeze fast path) are heavier to embed in a plain Python service than `transformers`-native models (Qwen3-TTS, MOSS, CSM, VibeVoice) or pip-packaged ones (kokoro, chatterbox-tts, hume-tada, dia2).
- Dormant upstreams (Kokoro, Step Audio EditX, Zonos) mean pinning exact package versions and vendoring inference code is advisable when registering in MLflow.

### Gaps
- No independent, like-for-like latency benchmark across the 2026 models on the same GPU was found; vendor numbers use H100/H200/L40S/4090 inconsistently.
- Peak VRAM for Fish S2 Pro, Higgs TTS 3, Magpie and Chatterbox is not published.
- No sources on Python dependency conflict matrices (e.g., torch/transformers pins) across models.

## Key Question 4: Known gotchas (hallucination, chunking, normalization, watermarking, license terms on generated audio)

### Takeaway
Autoregressive LLM-TTS models (the whole 2025-2026 top tier) skip or hallucinate on long inputs -- Qwen3-TTS-0.6B WER jumps from 5.4% under 500 words to 35.2% above 1500 words -- so production services must chunk or use error-detect-and-regenerate. Chatterbox, VibeVoice and NeuTTS embed watermarks in every output; Breeze, Fish, Voxtral, Higgs TTS 3, F5-TTS, Spark, Llasa and XTTS restrict commercial use of self-hosted outputs; Kokoro has a 512-token (~30 s) window and needs external chunking and number normalization.

### Cited Findings
- Long-text failure: "With Qwen3-TTS-0.6B, the word error rate on prompts with fewer than 500 words is 5.4%, but this increases to 35.2% on concatenated prompts longer than 1500 words"; the paper "Taming Long-form Text-to-Speech" (Sep 15 2026) introduces Localized Attention-Constrained Inference (LACI) that detects errors in near real time, rolls back and regenerates, cutting long-form WER to 3.4%; tested on Qwen3-TTS-0.6B and VoxCPM2 — [arXiv 2609.16989](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16989)
- Failure taxonomy for AR TTS: "skip (a contiguous deletion of 10 or more words) and hallucination (a contiguous mix of substitutions and insertions of 20 or more words)"; AR encoder-decoder models "suffer from hallucination, skipping or repeating words" — [arXiv 2609.16989 via search](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.16989); [GFlowNets hallucination paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.15442)
- F5-TTS "hallucinates on long texts" (e.g., generating different content than the reference text) — [F5-TTS discussions](https://github.com/SWivid/F5-TTS/discussions/129); Orpheus hallucination at end of utterance reported (Korean fine-tune) — [Orpheus issue #276](https://github.com/canopyai/Orpheus-TTS/issues/276)
- Hume TADA positions itself as the fix: "Zero hallucinations" across 1000+ LibriTTS-R samples (CER threshold 0.15) via 1:1 text-acoustic token alignment — [Hume blog](https://www.hume.ai/blog/opensource-tada)
- Chatterbox-Multilingual V3 advertises "reduced hallucinations"; reference audio must match the language tag or set CFG to 0 to avoid accent transfer; higher `exaggeration` speeds up speech — [resemble-ai/chatterbox](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox); [ResembleAI/chatterbox](https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox)
- Kokoro chunking: 512-token window (~30 s audio); >510 phonemes throws an error and requires splitting; recommended <500 chars per chunk; Kokoro-FastAPI added "improved text normalization for numbers, currency, and time formats" and has "a pinned long-number failure in the accepted test cases" — [deAPI Kokoro guide](https://deapi.ai/blog/kokoro-tts-guide-how-to-control-41-voices-with-nothing-but-punctuation); [FluidAudio issue #940](https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio/issues/940); [Kokoro-FastAPI CHANGELOG](https://github.com/remsky/Kokoro-FastAPI/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md); Kokoro card has "limited explicit documentation on number/digit handling" — [hexgrad/Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)
- Per-request length caps: Step Audio EditX ~30 s audio per inference; Magpie ~20 s cap; Dia2 2 min max; Llasa 2048 tokens; Kyutai audio lags text by 1.28 s — [stepfun-ai/Step-Audio-EditX](https://huggingface.co/stepfun-ai/Step-Audio-EditX); [Pinggy blog](https://pinggy.io/blog/best_open_source_self_hosted_text_to_speech_models/); [nari-labs/Dia2-2B](https://huggingface.co/nari-labs/Dia2-2B); [HKUSTAudio/Llasa-3B](https://huggingface.co/HKUSTAudio/Llasa-3B); [kyutai/tts-1.6b-en_fr](https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-1.6b-en_fr)
- Watermarking: Chatterbox (all variants) embeds Resemble's PerTh neural watermark, imperceptible, survives MP3/editing, ~100% detection — [Resemble Chatterbox Multilingual v3](https://www.resemble.ai/resources/chatterbox-multilingual-v3-tts-with-embedded-watermarking-for-25-languages); VibeVoice adds an audible AI disclaimer plus imperceptible watermark to every file — [microsoft/VibeVoice-1.5B](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/VibeVoice-1.5B); NeuTTS Air (0.5B, Neuphonic) also PerTh-watermarked — [BentoML blog](https://www.bentoml.com/blog/exploring-the-world-of-open-source-text-to-speech-models); Kyutai TTS "does not perform watermarking" but restricts to pre-computed voice embeddings — [kyutai/tts-1.6b-en_fr](https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-1.6b-en_fr)
- License terms on generated audio: Breeze TTS 2 -- "self-hosted outputs are governed separately by the BreezeBlue Research and Non-Commercial License"; a paid subscription "does not grant commercial rights to the open-weight model or self-hosted outputs" — [MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/breeze-tts-2-license-commercial-use); Higgs TTS 3 -- creators may monetize outputs only with attribution to "Boson AI's Higgs Audio" — [bosonai/higgs-audio-v3-tts-4b](https://huggingface.co/bosonai/higgs-audio-v3-tts-4b); F5-TTS -- CC-BY-NC base "cannot be used commercially even after finetuning" — [F5-TTS discussion #997](https://github.com/SWivid/F5-TTS/discussions/997); Voxtral -- CC BY-NC 4.0, "You cannot ship a product with it or use it in a business workflow without licensing it through Mistral's API" — [Novaknown](https://novaknown.com/2026/03/27/voxtral-tts-open-weights/); XTTS-v2 -- CPML non-commercial — [coqui/XTTS-v2](https://huggingface.co/coqui/XTTS-v2)
- "The GitHub badge says Apache 2.0 for models you cannot ship commercially" -- the top two open-weight arena models (Breeze, Fish) are non-commercial despite open branding — [Pinggy blog](https://pinggy.io/blog/best_open_source_self_hosted_text_to_speech_models/)
- Step Audio EditX license ambiguity: model card says Apache 2.0, but a user found checkpoint text specifying "academic research purposes"; StepFun replied only that "license terms and corresponding obligations are governed by the license itself" — [HF discussion](https://huggingface.co/stepfun-ai/Step-Audio-EditX/discussions/1)
- Fun-CosyVoice3 card disclaimer says the release is "for academic purposes" despite Apache 2.0 — [FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512](https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512)
- Responsible-use clauses that are conditions of the license, not just guidance: VibeVoice "not intended or licensed" for impersonation, disinformation, real-time deepfakes, or non-EN/ZH content — [microsoft/VibeVoice-1.5B](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/VibeVoice-1.5B); Sesame CSM requires explicit consent before mimicking real individuals — [sesame/csm-1b](https://huggingface.co/sesame/csm-1b); Higgs TTS 3 prohibits non-consensual cloning, election deception, biometric surveillance — [bosonai/higgs-audio-v3-tts-4b](https://huggingface.co/bosonai/higgs-audio-v3-tts-4b)
- Kokoro: "Watch for scam websites claiming official status" — [BentoML blog](https://www.bentoml.com/blog/exploring-the-world-of-open-source-text-to-speech-models)
- Higgs TTS 3 sound effects require paired onomatopoeia (e.g. `<|sfx:laughter|>Haha`); reference transcript improves cloning — [bosonai/higgs-audio-v3-tts-4b](https://huggingface.co/bosonai/higgs-audio-v3-tts-4b)
- Dia2: inconsistent nonverbal-tag handling, no fixed voice identity; ChatTTS: stability issues — [BentoML blog](https://www.bentoml.com/blog/exploring-the-world-of-open-source-text-to-speech-models)
- MOSS-TTS v1.5: language tags recommended for non-ZH/EN text; cuDNN SDPA backend must be disabled — [OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-v1.5](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-v1.5)

### Inferences
- Any production service should sentence-split and chunk to <500 chars (Kokoro) or well under the model's per-request cap (30 s for Step Audio EditX, 20 s Magpie) and do its own number/abbreviation normalization upstream, because model cards rarely document normalizer coverage.
- If output must be watermark-free (e.g., for downstream audio processing or privacy reasons), Chatterbox and VibeVoice are excluded; Kokoro, Qwen3-TTS, CosyVoice3, Kyutai carry no documented watermark (absence of documentation, not confirmation).
- MLflow registry metadata should store the weight-repo license string and commit hash, since several repos (Step Audio EditX, CosyVoice3, IndexTTS) have code/weight license inconsistencies that could change.

### Gaps
- No systematic measurement of hallucination rate for Chatterbox, Kokoro, CosyVoice3 or Step Audio EditX; only anecdotal reports and vendor claims.
- No documentation found on text-normalization (numbers, abbreviations, dates) coverage for Qwen3-TTS, Chatterbox, CosyVoice3 or Fish S2 Pro.
- Whether Qwen3-TTS, Fun-CosyVoice3, Fish S2 Pro or Breeze embed any watermark is not stated on their cards (not confirmed either way).
