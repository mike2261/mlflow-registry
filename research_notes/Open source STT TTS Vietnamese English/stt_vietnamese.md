# Open-weight Vietnamese speech-to-text (STT / ASR) models — state as of September 2026

Scope: open-weight, self-hostable ASR models that handle Vietnamese, with concrete WER numbers, licenses, sizes, Hugging Face repo ids and deployment notes. Written for an engineer who will pull weights from Hugging Face, store them in MinIO, register them in MLflow and serve them from a Python service. English-only coverage is out of scope.

Reading guide for the numbers: Vietnamese WER figures below come from different papers/model cards using different text normalizations, so cross-source comparisons are only indicative. Where the same source reports several models on the same set (ChunkFormer card, PhoASR paper, ViMD paper, ViMedCSS paper, GigaSpeech 2 paper) those rows are directly comparable and are marked as such.

## Key question 1: Which open-weight models give the best Vietnamese WER today (Sept 2026)?

### Takeaway
There is no single leaderboard that ranks all candidates on Vietnamese; the best evidence is a patchwork of model cards and 2025–2026 papers. On clean/read speech the strongest open weights are (a) Alibaba's **Qwen3-ASR-1.7B** (Apache-2.0, Jan 2026; FLEURS-vi WER 5.55), (b) NVIDIA's **parakeet-ctc-0.6b-Vietnamese** (NVIDIA Open Model License; FLEURS 5.15, VIVOS 5.96, Common Voice 9.73, with punctuation and streaming), (c) Zalo-affiliated **ChunkFormer-large-vie** (CC-BY-NC-4.0, 110M; VIVOS 4.18 / CV 6.66) and (d) VinAI's older **PhoWhisper-large** (BSD-3-Clause, 1.55B; VIVOS 4.67 / CV 8.14). Vanilla Whisper large-v3 is clearly behind on Vietnamese (FLEURS ~8.3–8.6, Common Voice ~13.7–16.2, VIVOS 8.8–12.3) and Meta's Omnilingual ASR supports Vietnamese under Apache-2.0 but publishes no Vietnamese-specific score.

- Whisper large-v3 Vietnamese WER as measured by the GigaSpeech 2 authors: Common Voice vi 13.74, FLEURS vi 8.59, GigaSpeech 2 vi test 17.94 — [GigaSpeech 2 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.11546)
- Whisper large-v3 Vietnamese WER as measured on the ChunkFormer card (same normalisation as ChunkFormer/PhoWhisper rows): VIVOS 8.81, Common Voice 15.45, VLSP2020 Task 1 20.41, avg 14.89 — [khanhld/chunkformer-large-vie](https://huggingface.co/khanhld/chunkformer-large-vie)
- Whisper large-v3 Vietnamese WER as measured by an independent fine-tuner: Common Voice v16.1 16.2, FLEURS 8.3, VIVOS 12.3 — [doof-ferb/whisper-large-peft-lora-vi](https://huggingface.co/doof-ferb/whisper-large-peft-lora-vi)

### Inferences
- For a single general-purpose Vietnamese model with permissive licensing and the best documented accuracy across many public test sets, **parakeet-ctc-0.6b-Vietnamese** (with punctuation, timestamps, streaming) and **Qwen3-ASR-1.7B** (Apache-2.0, FLEURS-vi 5.55) are the two front-runners; Qwen3-ASR is a decoder-based LLM-style model (heavier, needs vLLM for streaming), Parakeet is a CTC model (fast, greedy decode, .nemo format via NeMo).
- **ChunkFormer-large-vie** is the accuracy/size sweet spot (110M, beats PhoWhisper-large on average) but its CC-BY-NC-4.0 license blocks commercial deployment; PhoWhisper (BSD-3-Clause) is permissive but is a 2024 model with no punctuation and roughly 2× the error of the best 2026 systems on spontaneous speech.
- Qwen3-ASR's FLEURS-vi 5.55 vs Parakeet's 5.15 vs BuzzASR's 7.26 vs Whisper-large-v3's ~8.3–8.6 suggests the FLEURS read-speech gap between the best open models is small; differentiation is on spontaneous/noisy data (GigaSpeech 2, VLSP T2, VietMed), where only Parakeet and PhoASR publish numbers.
- Omnilingual ASR should be treated as "supports Vietnamese, accuracy unknown" until someone benchmarks it; the LLM-7B variant's 30 GiB download and RTF ~0.09 make it a poor default for a Python microservice compared to 0.6B–1.7B alternatives.

### Gaps
- No public, up-to-date leaderboard ranks these models on Vietnamese: the Open ASR Leaderboard's multilingual track covers only DE/FR/IT/ES/PT — [HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/open-asr-leaderboard); the community "Vietnamese Open ASR Leaderboard" Space exists (9 test sets, 211 h, O-WER/N-WER columns) but its `data.xlsx` results file contained only empty column headers when downloaded on 26 Sep 2026 — [VietAudio-team dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/VietAudio-team/Vietnamese-asr-leaderboard); [Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/VietAudio-team/Vietnamese-ASR-Leaderboard)
- Omnilingual ASR: no Vietnamese CER/WER found in the model card or paper text.
- Qwen3-ASR: no Common Voice-vi, VIVOS or VLSP numbers; no side-by-side with Whisper on Vietnamese.
- VietASR: HF repo id / license not located.
- Whether Qwen-Audio-3.0-ASR (Sept 2026) is open-weight and covers Vietnamese is unverified.
- Gemma 3n/4 Vietnamese ASR quality: no official per-language numbers; SEA-SpeechBench suggests very poor zero-shot Vietnamese ASR for Gemma-3n.

## Key question 3: Vietnamese-specific fine-tunes vs big multilingual models — tones, dialects (Northern/Central/Southern), English code-switching

### Takeaway
Vietnamese-specialised models (PhoWhisper, ChunkFormer, Parakeet-vi, VietASR) consistently halve Whisper-large-v3's error on general Vietnamese, but the picture flips on English insertions: on the ViMedCSS code-switching benchmark Whisper-large-v3 has the lowest error on embedded English terms (CS-WER 46.69) while PhoWhisper-large and VietASR have the lowest overall WER. Central-region dialects are the hardest for every model tested (ViMD: PhoWhisper-base zero-shot 14.96 North vs 24.15 Central vs 17.87 South), and PhoASR's authors explicitly warn their model is weaker on Central speakers.

### Cited Findings
- ViMD (EMNLP 2024; 102.56 h, ~19k utterances, 63 provincial dialects; CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0 on HF) zero-shot WER North / Central / South / Overall: PhoWhisper-base 14.96 / 24.15 / 17.87 / 18.61; whisper-base 26.37 / 39.91 / 29.46 / 31.38; wav2vec2-base-vietnamese(-250h) 20.32 / 27.28 / 22.48 / 23.07; wav2vec2-base-vietnamese-160h 27.50 / 38.12 / 30.93 / 31.74. After fine-tuning on ViMD: wav2vec2-base-vi-vlsp2020 12.17 / 17.19 / 15.08 / 12.24; PhoWhisper-base 13.20 / 18.26 / 13.54 / 16.30; whisper-base 20.05 / 27.89 / 20.89 / 19.93. "The Central region consistently demonstrated the highest error rates", attributed to distinct vowel shifts (e.g., 'a' → 'o') — [ViMD paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.03458); [ViMD dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nguyendv02/ViMD_Dataset)
- PhoASR training data: "Northern accent is the most dominant, followed by the Central accent, while the Southern accent is the least represented"; a region-balanced 192 h subset (64 h per region) outperformed single-dialect training — [PhoASR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14779); the released PhoASR-whisper-small "may show reduced accuracy for Central Vietnamese speakers and other underrepresented dialects" — [PhoASR HF card](https://huggingface.co/Qualcomm-AI-Research/PhoASR-whisper-small)
- PhoWhisper's training set includes recordings from "26K people spanning 63 provinces", i.e. it was built for accent coverage, but the paper reports no per-dialect breakdown — [PhoWhisper paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02555)
- Parakeet-vi reports ViMD (multi-dialect) WER 11.02 as a blind test — [nvidia/parakeet-ctc-0.6b-Vietnamese](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-ctc-0.6b-Vietnamese)
- VietLyrics (Whisper-based lyrics transcription) notes its model "performs worse on female Southern dialect" and that errors arise "from character or diacritic replacements" — [VietLyrics](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.22295v2)
- Code-switching (ViMedCSS, LREC 2026; 34 h, 16,576 utterances with English medical terms; CC-BY-4.0): zero-shot WER / CS-WER on the test split: VietASR 27.56 / 58.38; PhoWhisper-Large 31.24 / 55.05; Whisper-Large-v3 34.47 / 46.69; PhoWhisper-Small 36.31 / 62.55; Whisper-Small 50.03 / 61.25; wav2vec2-base-vi 44.74 / 69.05. Authors: "monolingual or Vietnamese-adapted models dominate sentence-level accuracy, whereas broad multilingual exposure improves recognition of embedded English 'islands'". Fine-tuning PhoWhisper-small with an attention-guide adaptation cut WER to 23.67 and CS-WER to 19.50 — [ViMedCSS](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12911)
- Parakeet-vi is marketed as a "Vietnamese English code switch model, mainly focused on Vietnamese" that "can transcribe some popular English words, but not all English audio" — [NGC catalog](https://catalog.ngc.nvidia.com/orgs/nim/teams/nvidia/containers/parakeet-ctc-0.6b-vi)
- VLSP 2025 organisers list "tonal characteristics and dialectal variations" as the Vietnamese-specific challenges and recommend "data quality over quantity" — [VLSP 2025 overview](https://aclanthology.org/2025.vlsp-1.1.pdf)
- VLSP 2025 4th-place team filtered training data by "requiring Vietnamese tone marks" (dropping samples lacking diacritics) — [VLSP 2025 overview](https://aclanthology.org/2025.vlsp-1.1.pdf)
- Vietnamese-adapted PhoWhisper-base is also a strong dialect *identifier* (F1-macro 0.8697 vs whisper-base 0.8559 on ViMD) — [ViMD paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03458)

### Inferences
- Tones are handled implicitly by every model (output is fully-diacritised Vietnamese orthography); the practical risk is diacritic/tone-mark substitution errors on Central dialects and noisy audio, which N-WER hides but O-WER and downstream NLU expose.
- If the product audience is Southern (Ho Chi Minh City) or Central (Huế/Đà Nẵng), evaluate on ViMD's regional splits before choosing; Parakeet-vi is the only 2026 model with a published ViMD number.
- For heavy Vietnamese–English mixing (tech support, medical), a Whisper-derived model (multilingual prior retained) or Parakeet-vi's explicit code-switch training is safer than CTC models trained on purely Vietnamese text; a domain fine-tune on even ~30 h of code-switched data gives large CS-WER gains (ViMedCSS: 62.6 → 19.5).

### Gaps
- No dialect-split numbers for Qwen3-ASR, Omnilingual ASR, ChunkFormer or PhoWhisper-large.
- No code-switching numbers for Parakeet-vi, ChunkFormer or Qwen3-ASR (ViMedCSS did not test them).
- No study found on Southern vs Northern *lexical* variation (e.g., regional vocabulary) affecting ASR output.

## Key question 4: Known gotchas — diacritics, punctuation/casing, hallucination, dataset licensing

### Takeaway
Most classic Vietnamese benchmarks (VIVOS, Common Voice, VLSP2020) are lower-cased, unpunctuated text, so older models (PhoWhisper, wav2vec2, ChunkFormer) emit no punctuation/casing and their WERs are "normalised" numbers; the 2026 generation (Parakeet-vi, PhoASR, Qwen3-ASR) outputs punctuation and casing. Whisper-family models hallucinate on silence/non-speech and repeat; several of the training/eval corpora and two strong models (ChunkFormer, wav2vec2-250h) are non-commercial.

### Cited Findings
- Whisper model card: "predictions may include texts that are not actually spoken in the audio input (i.e. hallucination)" and the seq2seq model "is prone to generating repetitive texts" — [openai/whisper-large-v3](https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3); Vietnamese lyrics work documents "hallucinations in non-vocal segments" — [VietLyrics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.22295)
- Existing Vietnamese datasets suffer "lack of punctuation, capitalization, word-level timestamps", inconsistent transcripts and 8–48 kHz sample rates; manual audit found original transcripts averaged 2.73 % WER (VietMed-L 14.89 %) vs 0.23 % after PhoASR cleaning; only ~62 % of sampled data survived filtering — [PhoASR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14779)
- O-WER vs N-WER gap is huge for older models: ChunkFormer-large-vi 32.43 % O-WER vs 6.89 % N-WER; PhoWhisper-small 33.90 % vs 8.97 % (i.e., they produce no punctuation/casing) — [PhoASR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14779)
- Parakeet-vi's punctuation/capitalisation training labels were machine-generated with Qwen3 (except FLEURS) — [nvidia/parakeet-ctc-0.6b-Vietnamese](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-ctc-0.6b-Vietnamese)
- The community Vietnamese leaderboard normalises by preserving diacritics, spelling out numbers ("20" → "hai mươi") and keeping only comma, period, question and exclamation marks — [VietAudio-team leaderboard dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/VietAudio-team/Vietnamese-asr-leaderboard)
- PhoWhisper paper reports the wav2vec2 baselines at >100 % WER on Common Voice (103.71 / 101.41), signalling a normalisation/mismatch artefact rather than true accuracy — [PhoWhisper paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02555)
- Naive LoRA fine-tuning can regress: doof-ferb's Whisper-large-v3 LoRA improved CV and VIVOS but worsened FLEURS from 8.3 to 14.7 — [doof-ferb/whisper-large-peft-lora-vi](https://huggingface.co/doof-ferb/whisper-large-peft-lora-vi)
- BuzzASR/vietnamese was trained on FLEURS, so its FLEURS-vi 7.26 WER is in-domain — [BuzzASR/vietnamese](https://huggingface.co/BuzzASR/vietnamese)
- Common Voice Vietnamese is tiny: the leaderboard's CV test slice is 1,281 clips / 1 h 21 m; VIVOS test 1,000 clips / 1 h 14 m (both labelled "synthetic"/read) — [VietAudio-team leaderboard dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/VietAudio-team/Vietnamese-asr-leaderboard)
- Wav2vec2-250h accepts at most 10 s of audio per call — [nguyenvulebinh/wav2vec2-base-vietnamese-250h](https://huggingface.co/nguyenvulebinh/wav2vec2-base-vietnamese-250h); Whisper-family models work on 30 s windows; Gemma audio is capped at 30 s — [Gemma audio docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/capabilities/audio)
- Dataset licences: Bud500 (~500 h) Apache-2.0 — [Bud500 GitHub](https://github.com/apluka34/Bud500); viVoice (~1,000 h YouTube) CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, research only — [viVoice GitHub](https://github.com/dieptran43/viVoice); GigaSpeech 2 Vietnamese (~6,000–7,300 h) "non-commercial research and educational purposes only" — [GigaSpeech 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.11546); ViMD CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0 (HF card) / CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 (paper) — [ViMD HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nguyendv02/ViMD_Dataset), [ViMD paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.03458); ViMedCSS CC-BY-4.0 with YouTube audio "for research purposes only" — [ViMedCSS](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12911); PhoASR source datasets mix "Apache 2.0, CC-BY variants, and custom research licenses" — [PhoASR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14779)
- Model licences that block commercial use: ChunkFormer-large-vie CC-BY-NC-4.0 — [HF](https://huggingface.co/khanhld/chunkformer-large-vie); wav2vec2-base-vietnamese-250h CC-BY-NC-4.0 — [HF](https://huggingface.co/nguyenvulebinh/wav2vec2-base-vietnamese-250h); MMS-1b-all CC-BY-NC-4.0 — [HF](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-1b-all); SeamlessM4T-v2 CC-BY-NC-4.0 — [HF](https://huggingface.co/facebook/seamless-m4t-v2-large)
- Omnilingual ASR licence conflict: HF model card says Apache-2.0 for model and code — [facebook/omniASR-LLM-7B](https://huggingface.co/facebook/omniASR-LLM-7B) — while the arXiv HTML states artifacts are "under CC BY-SA 4.0" (likely the paper's own licence) — [Omnilingual ASR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.09690v1); the HF card should govern the weights.
- ChunkFormer training-hours conflict: its card says ~3,000 h — [HF](https://huggingface.co/khanhld/chunkformer-large-vie) — while the PhoASR paper labels it "ChunkFormer-large-vi (25K hrs)" — [PhoASR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14779)

### Inferences
- Because Vietnamese-specific models were trained on datasets that are themselves partly non-commercial (viVoice, GigaSpeech 2, VLSP research data), a permissive model licence does not guarantee the training data was cleared for commercial use; for a commercial service prefer models whose authors assert commercial rights (NVIDIA Open Model License, Apache-2.0 Qwen3-ASR, BSD PhoWhisper) and keep the licence text in the MLflow model artifact.
- Any evaluation harness should compute both an orthographic and a normalised WER (diacritics preserved, digits spelled out, punctuation stripped) so that punctuation-free models and punctuating models are compared fairly; the VietAudio normalisation rules are a reasonable template.
- Add a VAD front-end (e.g., Silero) before Whisper-family models to suppress hallucination on silence; CTC models (Parakeet, ChunkFormer) are structurally less prone to hallucination but can drop diacritics on Central-accent speech.

### Gaps
- No systematic Vietnamese-specific hallucination-rate study was found (only anecdotal mentions in VietLyrics and general Whisper hallucination papers).
- Whether Parakeet-vi's Qwen3-generated punctuation labels introduce systematic punctuation errors is not reported.
- Exact licences of VLSP 2020/2021/2023 datasets (used to train PhoWhisper, wav2vec2-250h, Parakeet-vi) are not stated in any fetched source.

## Key question 5: What benchmark infrastructure exists for choosing/validating a Vietnamese model?

### Takeaway
Standard Vietnamese sets are VIVOS (read, ~1 h test), Common Voice vi (tiny), FLEURS-vi (~12 h read), VLSP 2020/2021 tasks (T1 clean, T2 noisy/spontaneous), Bud500 (Apache-2.0 spontaneous), ViMD (dialects), GigaSpeech 2 vi (YouTube), LSVSC, FOSD, VietMed; the PhoASR test set and the VietAudio leaderboard bundle aggregate most of these with clean normalisation.

### Cited Findings
- FLEURS covers 102 languages with about 12 h of Vietnamese read speech — [Open ASR Leaderboard paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06961v1)
- VietAudio leaderboard ground truth: viVoice 100k clips/114 h, LSVSC 11,364/20 h, VLSP 11,285/19 h, Bud500 14,999/10.7 h, GigaSpeech2 8,844/17.5 h, FOSD 5,183/7 h, ViMD 3,799/20.4 h, Common Voice 1,281/1.35 h, VIVOS 1,000/1.2 h (157,755 clips, 211+ h) — [VietAudio-team dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/VietAudio-team/Vietnamese-asr-leaderboard)
- PhoASR aggregated 11 sources (CMV-vi-14, VIVOS, FOSD, VSV-1100, viVoice, BUD500, VLSP2020, Vietnam-Celeb, ViMD, LSVSC, VietMed-L) into 502.67 h (468.6 train / 18.7 val / 15.4 test) plus a 3,100 h training set — [PhoASR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14779)
- VietSuperSpeech (Mar 2026): 267.39 h of conversational YouTube Vietnamese (240.67 h train / 26.72 h test) pseudo-labelled with a "Zipformer-30M-RNNT-6000h" model; positioned for call-centre/chatbot fine-tuning — [VietSuperSpeech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01894)
- Bud500 spans "Vietnamese accents from all regions" and topics (podcast, travel, book, food) under Apache-2.0 — [Bud500 GitHub](https://github.com/apluka34/Bud500)
- VLSP 2025 used Syllable Error Rate (SyER) as the ASR metric and allowed external pre-trained models and open datasets — [VLSP 2025 overview](https://aclanthology.org/2025.vlsp-1.1.pdf)

### Inferences
- A practical internal eval for the MLflow registry: FLEURS-vi test + VIVOS test (read speech, both freely redistributable), Bud500 test (spontaneous, Apache-2.0) and ViMD test (dialects, non-commercial but fine for internal eval), scored with VietAudio-style normalisation; this reproduces most numbers cited above.

### Gaps
- The VietAudio leaderboard has no published scores yet (empty results file on 26 Sep 2026), so no third-party ranking of Qwen3-ASR vs Parakeet-vi vs ChunkFormer on identical normalisation exists.
- The "Zipformer-30M-RNNT-6000h" model referenced by VietSuperSpeech and the VLSP 2025 winning Zipformer do not appear to have public weights (none found).
